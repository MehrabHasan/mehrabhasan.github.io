About me

I am Mehrab Hasan, a PhD candidate in the School of Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering at the University of Oklahoma, planning to graduate in 2028. I have been working here as a Graduate Research Assistant in the Smart Materials and Intelligent Systems Lab since 2025. My PhD research focuses on the fabrication and additive manufacturing of multifunctional composites, smart materials, and piezoresistive strain sensors for structural health monitoring. I also develop AI/ML-driven performance prediction models for smart materials. As a Graduate Teaching Assistant in the same school, I also mentor undergraduate students in Solid Mechanics, Aerodynamics, and Design of Mechanical Components courses.

Previously, I completed my MS in Materials Science and Engineering at the University of North Texas, USA, in Summer 2024, where I gained extensive experimental research experience in next-generation Li-ion and beyond-Li-ion battery development, including Metal–CO2 cells, additive manufacturing of Zn–V2O5 cells, and PVDF–MoS2 enhanced piezoelectric sensor development.
