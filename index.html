About Me

Background

I am a PhD researcher in the School of Aerospace & Mechanical Engineering at the University of Oklahoma. My work lies at the convergence of experimental materials science, electrochemical energy systems, and data-driven modeling.

My current research spans shape memory polymer composites — where I develop ML-assisted models for predicting shape recovery behavior. I had previous experience working in the development of lithium-ion / lithium-metal battery systems, including physics-based degradation modeling and model-predictive control strategies.

Previously at the University of North Texas, I worked on next-generation Metal–CO₂ batteries with 2D transition-metal dichalcogenide catalysts and additively manufactured Zn–V₂O₅ batteries, gaining deep expertise in electrochemical characterization and functional material fabrication.
