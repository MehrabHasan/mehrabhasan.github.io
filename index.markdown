# Mehrab Hasan

PhD researcher in Aerospace & Mechanical Engineering at the University of Oklahoma, 
working on smart materials, lithium battery modeling, and physics-informed machine learning.

---

## Research Interests
- Shape memory polymer and carbon-fiber reinforced composites  
- Lithium-ion and lithium-metal battery modeling and control  
- Physics-based and machine-learning-assisted predictive modeling  
- Additive manufacturing of functional materials  

---

## Research Experience

### University of Oklahoma
**Graduate Research Assistant (2024–Present)**  
- Developed ML-assisted shape recovery prediction models for shape memory epoxy composites  
- Designed experimental setups for Li-ion pouch cell disassembly and coin cell fabrication  
- Developed physics-based degradation models for graphite–silicon composite electrodes  
- Implemented MPC for Li-metal batteries with voltage, temperature, and electrolyte constraints  

### University of North Texas
**Graduate Research Assistant (2023–2024)**  
- Developed Iron–CO₂ batteries with 2D TMD catalysts achieving ~900% capacity improvement  
- Performed EIS, CV, and long-cycle testing for electrochemical performance evaluation  
- Fabricated additively manufactured Zn–V₂O₅ batteries and PVDF–MoSe₂ piezoelectric sensors
